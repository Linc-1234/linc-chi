What is 3D Design?

Understanding the foundation of digital 3D creation

3D design is the process of using specialized computer software to create digital models of objects or environments that possess three physical dimensions: height, width, and depth.

Unlike flat drawings, these virtual models can be viewed, rotated, and analyzed from any angle. Industries such as architecture, gaming, engineering, product design and film use 3D design to visualize concepts, test prototypes and create realistic simulations.

3D design uses computer software to build mathematical, three-dimensional representations of objects, characters, environments and surfaces.

Core Geometry & Structure

The building blocks of a 3D model

01 / GEOMETRY

Vertices, Edges & Faces

A 3D model is built from vertices, which are points in 3D space, edges which connect those points, and faces which form the surfaces enclosed by the edges.

02 / STRUCTURE

Mesh & Topology

Connected polygons form a mesh. Good topology creates clean geometry and helps models behave properly during animation, deformation and shading.

03 / SPACE

X, Y & Z Axes

Objects exist in three-dimensional space defined by X, Y and Z axes. These axes control position, rotation and scale.

The 3D Modelling Process

From a simple shape to a finished scene

01 — Blockout

Start with simple shapes such as cubes, spheres or cylinders to establish basic proportions, composition and scale.

02 — Texturing & Lighting

Apply materials and virtual light sources to create colors, reflections, highlights, shadows and realistic surface details.

03 — Rendering

Generate the final image or animation from the completed 3D scene using a rendering engine.
